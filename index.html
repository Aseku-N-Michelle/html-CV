I'm a second-year student at Uganda Christian University, studying information technology. This is my personal website where I'm sharing a bit about myself, what I'm learning, and how to reach me.

About Me

I'm a motivated and hardworking second-year student who enjoys taking on new challenges, whether that's a difficult assignment, a group project with a tight deadline, or simply learning a new tool on my own. I'm a quick learner and detail-oriented, and I'd rather spend an extra hour making sure something is done properly than rush through it and leave gaps.

I discovered my interest in technology early on, drawn in by how much impact well-designed systems can have on everyday life — from something as simple as a well-organized website to the networks and security systems that keep entire organizations running safely. That curiosity is what led me to pursue Information Technology at Uganda Christian University, and it's a passion that keeps growing the more modules I complete and the more I get to build things with my own hands, like this website.

I work well both independently and as part of a team. Through group assignments and class projects, I've learned that clear communication, splitting tasks fairly, and following through on commitments make the biggest difference between a project that goes smoothly and one that falls apart under pressure. I try to be the kind of teammate who shows up prepared and keeps others updated rather than leaving things to the last minute.

I'm currently building a strong foundation across several areas of IT, including networking, cybersecurity fundamentals, database management, and hardware maintenance, alongside programming in Python and web development with HTML and CSS. I enjoy the process of figuring out how systems work under the surface, and I genuinely like the moment of troubleshooting a stubborn problem until it finally clicks into place — it's one of the reasons I chose this field in the first place.

My goal is to graduate with practical, job-ready skills rather than just theoretical knowledge, and to get there through internships, personal projects, and staying consistent with learning outside the classroom as well as inside it. I'm disciplined about setting small goals for myself, adaptable when plans change, and committed to putting in the work needed to grow — both as a student now and as an IT professional afterward.
